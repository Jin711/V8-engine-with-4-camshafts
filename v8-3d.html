Inside the glass

The block, heads and covers are drawn as glass; the crank, rods, pistons and valves stay solid so they read inside. Blue intake charge turns yellow as the piston compresses it, ignites red at the plug, cools to orange through the power stroke and leaves through the exhaust. The amber mist is port injection. The front chains run the four cams at 2:1, and the flywheel and ring gear sit at the rear of the crank.

Driving the model

The tachometer is fixed at the top left of the view; its needle and digital readout follow engine speed from 10 rpm at idle to the 600 rpm design maximum. The one-piece accelerator in the control row below the view is drawn from its left side, hinged at its top left: released to 70° it holds idle, and dragging it down opens the throttle fully. Let go and it springs back. Sound starts on your first pointer or key press, then rises in pitch and volume with rpm.

Engine audio: 3.5 L Rover V8, Collard, public domain; pitched and mixed live with rpm.
